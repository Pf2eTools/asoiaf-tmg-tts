Distances are always measured:
*FROM:* the very edge of the tray (the inside edge of the marker line lining the tray),
*TO:* the closer side edge of the appropriate marker line

(example image: https://i.imgur.com/jcE9qEd.png)

Marker 3d lines are 0.4 inches high and 0.03 inches thick.

Normal ruler ticks appear in 1 inch increments and are 1 inch long.
Every 5 inches the tick is large and crosses the whole ruler.
For small units (Solo, Warmachine) normal ruler ticks on all sides are only 0.75 inches long


Tray Sizes in inches:

-Infantry: 5.5 * 4.8
-Cavalry: 5.5 * 5
-Monster: 2.76 * 2.76
-Warmachine: 2.76 * 5.55

Correctly scaled (in mm) CAD STLs need to be scaled by 0.03936 in blender,
correctly positioned, then exported as .OBJ
